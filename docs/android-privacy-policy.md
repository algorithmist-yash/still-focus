# Still for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

Still is developed by **Yash Raj**. For privacy questions or app issues, contact **yash.algorithmist@gmail.com**. This is the developer's contact address; there is no separate support mailbox.

## Information stored on your device

Still stores session/event names, timer state, focus and break history, stopwatch laps, app selections, and activity filter preferences locally on your device. There is no Still account, cloud backup service, or cross-device synchronization operated by the developer. These records are not sent to the developer by the application.

## Installed apps and Accessibility permission

Still lists launchable apps so that you can choose distractions to restrict. If you grant Accessibility permission, its service receives foreground window events containing application identifiers. It uses these identifiers and your selections to display a blocking screen during a focus session. It does not request window-content retrieval and does not read screen text, messages, passwords, or keystrokes. Accessibility event information is not transmitted to the developer or used for advertising.

Permission is optional. Timers remain usable without it. You can turn the service off through Android Settings at any time. Still does not prevent you from changing permission settings or uninstalling it.

## External services and network resources

The bundled interface references Google Fonts. Where network access is available, loading fonts may send technical request information, such as an IP address, to Google. The Android manifest currently does not request Internet permission, so external resources may be unavailable in the native app. Android, Google Play, and any services you use to download the app have their own privacy practices. The linked GitHub privacy-policy page is hosted by GitHub and is subject to GitHub's policies.

Still includes no advertising, developer-operated analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. Activity analytics inside Still are calculated locally from your clock sessions.

## Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear Still's storage through Android Settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes session history, preferences, and selections. Still disables Android application backup in its manifest. The developer cannot retrieve or restore your local records.

## Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and message are received by the developer's email provider and used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may request deletion of correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

## Changes

This policy will be updated when Still's data handling changes. Its effective date will identify the latest version.
